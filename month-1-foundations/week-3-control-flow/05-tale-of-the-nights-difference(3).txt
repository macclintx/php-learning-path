night 4 is never counted because of where the night_told counter sits. Its after inner loop and break 2 jumps over
everything. In part 2, night 4 is counted because setting a flag only asks the loop to 
the next time it checks its condition.

In part one, the reader has to look inside both loops to know why the loop ends and in part two the reader has to look
at the condition for the loops.
